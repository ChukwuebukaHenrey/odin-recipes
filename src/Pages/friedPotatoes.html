Fried Potatoes

Crispy on the outside, tender on the inside. A simple side dish with familiar ingredients.

ABOUT THIS RECIPE

Description

Fried potatoes are a simple and delicious side dish made by frying sliced potatoes in oil until crispy. Salt, pepper and fresh scent leaves add flavour to this straightforward recipe.

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Scent leaves

LET'S GET COOKING

Preparation Steps

  1. Cut the potatoes into fry-shaped pieces.
  2. Heat oil in a deep pan or fryer to 375°F.
  3. Carefully add the potato pieces and fry until golden brown.
  4. Remove the potatoes and drain on paper towels.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Garnish with chopped scent leaves if desired.
