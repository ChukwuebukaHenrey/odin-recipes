Lasagna

Layers of pasta, savoury beef sauce and melted cheese. A comforting meal made for sharing.

ABOUT THIS RECIPE

Description

Lasagna is a type of pasta dish made with layers of flat noodles and sauce. This version combines ground beef, tomato sauce and mozzarella cheese for a rich, satisfying meal.

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese

LET'S GET COOKING

Preparation Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package directions.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a skillet.
  3. Add the tomato sauce to the beef and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Layer the noodles, beef sauce and cheese in a baking dish.
  5. Bake at 375°F for 30 minutes or until golden brown.
