Yam Casserole

A hearty yam dish with butter and cinnamon, prepared for a warm and comforting meal.

ABOUT THIS RECIPE

Description

Yam casserole is a traditional dish made with yams, often served during holidays. This recipe combines cooked yams with butter, sugar and cinnamon before baking until golden.

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • Yams
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Cinnamon

LET'S GET COOKING

Preparation Steps

  1. Cook the yams until tender.
  2. Melt butter in a saucepan and add sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Combine the cooked yams with the butter mixture.
  4. Pour the mixture into a baking dish.
  5. Bake at 375°F for 30 minutes or until golden brown.
